What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a commonly used measurement calculated from a person's height and weight to provide an estimate of whether their weight falls within a particular range.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

BMI is a general screening measurement and does not directly measure body fat or account for every individual health factor.

BMI Categories

BMI Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Normal weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 or higher Obesity

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For many adults, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is generally classified as the normal or healthy range.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI is a screening tool rather than a complete health assessment. It may not accurately represent body composition for athletes, children, older adults, or some other groups.

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.
