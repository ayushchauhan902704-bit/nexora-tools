FAQ - BMI Calculator

What is ideal BMI range?

18.5 to 24.9 is considered healthy for adults. Below 18.5 Underweight, above 25 Overweight.

How to calculate ideal weight?

Ideal Weight = 18.5 to 24.9 * height(m)². For 170cm, ideal weight 53.5kg to 72kg. Our calculator shows it.

Is BMI same for men and women?

Formula same for BMI calculator for men and BMI calculator for women. Category same, body composition differs.
