BMI Calculator - Body Mass Index

Calculate your BMI and check your ideal weight range instantly.

What is BMI Calculator?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple number that tells you if your weight is healthy for your height. Our free BMI Calculator uses the standard formula BMI = Weight(kg) / Height(m)² to give you instant results. For example, if you are 170cm tall and weigh 65kg, your BMI is 22.5, which falls in the healthy range.

How to Use This BMI Calculator?

Step 1: Enter your height in centimeters. Step 2: Enter your weight in kilograms. Step 3: Click Calculate. You will get your BMI value, health category (Underweight, Healthy, Overweight, Obese) and your ideal weight range for your height.

BMI Chart - Healthy Range

As per WHO guidelines: Below 18.5 is Underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 is Healthy, 25 to 29.9 is Overweight, and 30+ is Obese. This chart is same for adult men and women. For children, BMI is measured using age-based percentiles. This tool gives you a quick estimate - always consult a doctor for medical advice.

FAQ - BMI Calculator

What is ideal BMI range?

18.5 to 24.9 is considered healthy for adults.

How to calculate ideal weight?

Ideal Weight = 18.5 to 24.9 * height(m)². For 170cm, ideal weight 53.5kg to 72kg.

Is BMI same for men and women?

Formula is same for both. Health interpretation may vary slightly.
