About the Age Calculator

NEXORA's free age calculator calculates your age based on your date of birth and a selected calculation date.

How to use it

Enter your date of birth, choose the date you want to calculate your age as of, and tap Calculate Age.

What does it calculate?

The calculator shows your exact age in years, months and days, along with an estimate of your total days, weeks and hours.

Is this age calculator free?

Yes. NEXORA's Age Calculator is free to use and works directly in your browser.
