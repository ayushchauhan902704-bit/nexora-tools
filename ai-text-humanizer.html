About AI Text Humanizer by NEXORA

This tool helps you rewrite text to make it more natural and easy to read. It improves sentence flow, adds natural tone and fixes robotic phrasing. Useful for making content more engaging for readers.

How to use?

1. Paste your text. 2. Select a tone. 3. Click Humanize. The tool will rewrite your text with better readability while keeping the original meaning.

Is it free?

Yes, 100% free, works in your browser and does not save your text.
