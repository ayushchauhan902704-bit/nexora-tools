Quick Percentage Formula

Use this simple formula whenever you need to calculate a percentage.

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100
Percentage of Number = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number
Percentage Change = ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

How to Calculate Percentage?

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. It is commonly used for discounts, marks, statistics, business calculations, finance and everyday calculations.

Example: 20% of 500

To calculate 20% of 500, multiply 500 by 20 and divide by 100.

(20 ÷ 100) × 500 = 100

Example: What percentage is 25 of 100?

(25 ÷ 100) × 100 = 25%
