EMI means Equated Monthly Installment - fixed amount you pay every month to bank. Formula: EMI = P×R×(1+R)^N / ((1+R)^N-1) where P=Principal loan amount, R=Monthly interest rate (yearly/12/100), N=Number of months (years×12). Example: 5 Lakh loan, 9.5% interest, 5 years (60 months) = EMI ₹10,448, Total Payment ₹6,26,880, Interest ₹1,26,880.
