What is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator helps you quickly find the amount you save when a percentage discount is applied to an original price.

It also calculates the final price you need to pay after the discount is applied.

How to Calculate a Discount

Enter the original price of the product and the discount percentage. Then click the Calculate Discount button.

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount % ÷ 100

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

If a product costs $1,000 and has a 20% discount:

Discount = $1,000 × 20 ÷ 100 = $200

Final Price = $1,000 − $200 = $800

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 20% off a price?

Multiply the original price by 20 and divide the result by 100. Then subtract the discount amount from the original price.

How much is 10% off $100?

A 10% discount on $100 is $10, so the final price is $90.

Can I use this calculator for any currency?

Yes. The calculator works with any currency because it calculates using numbers and percentages.
