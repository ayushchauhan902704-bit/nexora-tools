Free Video Compressor Online

NEXORA provides a simple way to reduce video file size in your browser. Upload a video, choose a target size and select the quality and resolution that suit your needs. This video compressor can be useful when preparing videos for sharing, messaging, websites and online forms.

Compress videos for sharing

Large videos can take longer to upload and share. Reducing video size can make files easier to send through messaging apps, email and other platforms. Choose a suitable target size based on the file size limit of the service you are using.

Supported video files

You can select video files supported by your browser, including common formats such as MP4 and MOV. The formats available for export depend on your browser and device. The output format is shown after compression.

How to compress a video

  • Select a video from your device.
  • Choose a target size such as 5MB, 10MB, 20MB or 50MB.
  • Select a resolution and compression quality.
  • Press Compress Video and keep the page open.
  • Preview and download the compressed file.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is NEXORA Video Compressor free?

Yes. You can use this browser-based tool without paying for a subscription.

2. Can I compress a video to exactly 5MB or 10MB?

The selected size is a target, not an exact guarantee. The final file size depends on video duration, content, audio, browser and encoding support.

3. Will my video audio remain after compression?

The tool attempts to preserve audio. Audio processing depends on browser support, and some browsers may not support the required recording features.

4. Does this tool add a watermark?

No intentional watermark is added by this tool.

5. Can I use it on an iPhone or Android phone?

The page is designed for mobile screens. Actual compression support depends on the browser and available device memory.

6. Are my videos uploaded to NEXORA?

No. This tool processes the selected video in your browser. It does not send the video file to a NEXORA upload server.

7. Why is the output file larger than my selected target?

The browser recorder cannot guarantee an exact output size. Try a lower quality setting or a lower resolution to reduce the file further.

8. Why is compression not working on my device?

Some browsers do not support canvas recording, audio capture or the selected output format. Try an updated browser and a shorter video.
