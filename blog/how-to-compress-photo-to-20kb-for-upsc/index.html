Why UPSC Rejects 100KB Photo?

UPSC server stores lakhs of applications. If everyone uploads 1MB photo, server will crash. That's why they keep limit 20KB-50KB. Our tool reduces quality smartly without blur.

Pro Tips for UPSC Photo

  • Background should be white or light blue
  • Face should cover 75% of photo
  • Use JPG, not PNG
  • Don't use selfie - use passport photo
