Free Online Word Counter

NEXORA's free Word Counter helps you quickly count the number of words and characters in your text. It is useful for students, writers, bloggers, content creators, professionals and anyone who needs to check the length of written content.

Simply type or paste your text into the box above. The tool instantly calculates words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, reading time and speaking time.

What Does This Word Counter Calculate?

  • Total number of words
  • Total characters
  • Characters excluding spaces
  • Number of sentences
  • Number of paragraphs
  • Estimated reading time
  • Estimated speaking time

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. View the word and character count instantly.
  3. Check sentences and paragraphs if needed.
  4. Use the reading and speaking time estimates to understand how long your content may take.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Word Counter free?

Yes. NEXORA's Word Counter is free to use online.

Can I count characters too?

Yes. The tool shows total characters as well as characters without spaces.

Does the tool work on mobile phones?

Yes. The Word Counter is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and desktop devices.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. The counting calculations are performed directly in your browser on this page.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is an estimate based on an average reading speed of approximately 200 words per minute.
