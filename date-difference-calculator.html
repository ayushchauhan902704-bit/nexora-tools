What is a Date Difference Calculator?

A Date Difference Calculator helps you find the amount of time between two calendar dates. It can be useful for calculating project durations, age-related periods, deadlines, anniversaries, contracts and other date-based periods.

Enter a start date and an end date above to instantly calculate the difference.

How to Calculate the Difference Between Two Dates

Select the starting date and ending date, then click Calculate Difference.

The calculator shows the calendar difference in years, months and days, along with the total number of days, weeks, hours and minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator work with leap years?

Yes. The calendar calculation accounts for different month lengths and leap years.

Can I calculate the difference between past dates?

Yes. You can enter any two valid calendar dates.

Is my date information stored?

No. The calculation is performed directly in your browser.
