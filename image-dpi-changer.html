What is an Image DPI Changer?

DPI means dots per inch. It describes the density used when an image is printed. Changing DPI metadata can affect the intended physical print size while keeping the same pixel dimensions.

How to Change Image DPI

  • Upload your JPG or PNG image.
  • Choose 72, 150, 300, 600 or enter a custom DPI.
  • Click the Download Image button.
  • Your updated image will be saved to your device.

Why use 300 DPI?

300 DPI is commonly used as a print-resolution target for detailed printed images. The required DPI depends on the document, printer and actual pixel dimensions.

Does changing DPI improve image quality?

No. Changing DPI metadata alone does not add pixels or create new image detail.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG and PNG are supported. HEIC conversion is attempted using a browser-compatible converter. If conversion is unavailable, the tool will ask you to choose a JPG or PNG image instead.
