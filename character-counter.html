Free Online Character Counter

NEXORA's character counter helps you quickly count the number of characters in any text. It is useful for social media posts, essays, assignments, descriptions, titles and many other tasks.

The counter updates instantly while you type or paste text. Your text is processed directly in your browser.

What Does the Character Counter Count?

Characters

Counts every character in your text, including letters, numbers, punctuation and spaces.

Characters Without Spaces

Counts characters after removing spaces. This can be useful when a website or application has a character limit that excludes spaces.

Words

Counts the words contained in your text.

Sentences

Estimates the number of sentences based on common sentence ending punctuation such as periods, question marks and exclamation marks.

Paragraphs

Counts separate blocks of text divided by blank lines.

Why Use a Character Counter?

Character limits are common on social media, online forms, advertisements, titles and other digital platforms. A character counter makes it easy to check your text before publishing or submitting it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this character counter free?

Yes. NEXORA's character counter is free to use.

Does the counter work on mobile?

Yes. The tool is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and desktop devices.

Does my text get uploaded?

The counting calculations are performed directly in your browser. The tool does not need to upload your text to a server for counting.

Can I use it for social media text?

Yes. You can paste social media captions, descriptions, posts or other text to check its length.
