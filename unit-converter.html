What is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter helps you convert measurements from one unit to another. NEXORA's free unit converter supports commonly used units across several categories.

Supported Conversions

  • Length — meters, kilometers, centimeters, miles, feet, inches and more.
  • Weight — kilograms, grams, pounds, ounces and more.
  • Temperature — Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.
  • Area — square meters, square kilometers, acres and more.
  • Volume — liters, milliliters, gallons, cubic meters and more.
  • Speed — km/h, mph, m/s and knots.
  • Time — seconds, minutes, hours, days and weeks.
  • Data — bytes, KB, MB, GB and TB.
