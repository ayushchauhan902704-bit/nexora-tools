What is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter helps you convert measurements from one unit to another. NEXORA's free unit converter supports commonly used units.

Supported Conversions

  • Length — meters, kilometers, centimeters, miles, feet, inches
  • Weight — kilograms, grams, pounds, ounces
  • Temperature — Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin
  • Area — square meters, square kilometers, acres
  • Volume — liters, milliliters, gallons, cubic meters
  • Speed — km/h, mph, m/s, knots
  • Time — seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks
  • Data — bytes, KB, MB, GB, TB
